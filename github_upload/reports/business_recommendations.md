# Business follow-up / 业务建议

These are hypotheses for validation, not proven interventions. 以下建议是待验证的行动，不是已实现的留任收益。

|Evidence / 发现|Action to investigate / 待验证行动|Evaluation / 验证指标|
|---|---|---|
|Overtime: 30.53% attrition versus 10.44% without overtime. 加班组样本离职比例较高。|Investigate workload and scheduling within comparable roles. 在相近岗位内调查排班、工作负荷与管理支持。|Collect overtime hours, workload and dated attrition outcomes; evaluate a prospectively designed pilot. 收集工时与明确时间窗口，预先设计试点评估。|
|0–1 years at company: 75/215 leavers (34.88%). 新员工群体比例较高。|Review onboarding, mentoring and role expectations. 检查入职支持、导师机制和岗位预期。|Track cohorts from hire with consistent follow-up; compare onboarding completion and retention. 按入职批次追踪，统一随访时长。|
|Income medians differ, but job composition may explain part of the gap. 收入差异可能受岗位职级影响。|Audit compensation and career-development opportunities within comparable roles and levels. 在相同岗位职级内调查薪酬和发展机会。|Collect pay-band position, promotion opportunities and longitudinal outcomes. 补充薪酬带位置、晋升机会和纵向结果。|

The classifier should remain an educational benchmark. At threshold 0.5, the selected model misses 30 of 47 test-set leavers. It is not ready for individual employment decisions. 模型目前仅用于展示分析方法，不适合直接对员工做个体人事决策。
